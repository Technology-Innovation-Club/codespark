On starting young
“The first thing I needed was a room where the work was the point.”
Iyinoluwa Aboyeji, Founder, Andela & Flutterwave. Twelve years after his first company, he still builds with students. We are that room, for the next hundred founders.

CodeSpark measures what it funds. Six cohorts completed, fourteen startups incubated, three with paying customers within six months of the Forum. Two of them back as mentors.

The program takes no equity. The Hub is free for active participants. The grand prize is unrestricted. The forum recordings are public. We publish the full record because the work is the point.
